What happens during fire damper testing?+

We inspect and test the dampers within the agreed scope, record the findings and identify any follow-up work that may be needed.

How often should dampers be inspected?+

Schedules depend on the system and the requirements that apply to the building. Contact us and we can discuss the appropriate scope for your site.

Will I receive documentation?+

Inspection findings and relevant service documentation are provided for your building records.

Which areas do you cover?+

High Fire Heating is based in Knoxfield and services Melbourne and greater Victoria. Contact the team to confirm availability at your site.

How soon can the team attend?+

Timing depends on the site and current availability. Call if your request is urgent and the team can talk through the options.
