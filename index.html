What is backflow testing?+

Backflow testing checks that a prevention device is working properly to help protect the drinking water supply. APP Plumbing tests, documents and certifies the device against AS/NZS 2845.

Do I need an annual backflow test?+

Backflow devices require regular testing. If you’ve received a notice or are unsure of your due date, tell us the property suburb and we’ll help clarify the next step.

Do I get a compliance certificate?+

Yes. A compliance certificate is issued after testing, with same-day documentation.
